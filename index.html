Ask the barkeep

A few things worth knowing.

Never played Spider Solitaire before?

Build runs of cards in one suit, from King down to Ace. Clear the table to win. The illustrated owner's manual walks you through the rules in plain words.

Can I play without the internet?

Yes. The game works offline, with no account or advertising. Your name, scores, settings, and photos stay on your own device. Read the privacy policy for details.

Can I make things easier to see or quieter?

The game has Large Print card faces, high contrast, sound controls, and gentle motion options. The Back Room offers a quieter setting. You'll find the details in the manual.

Which Windows download should I choose?

Choose the installer for the usual setup with a desktop shortcut. Choose the all-in-one portable to play without installing. Both buttons download the game file directly.
